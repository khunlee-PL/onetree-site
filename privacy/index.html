Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

One Tree is free, has no ads, no subscriptions, no accounts, and no analytics or tracking. We do not run any server that receives your data. Everything the app does happens on your device, offline.

What the app stores on your device

Which branches you have visited, and a birth-year marker you may set in the deep-time tour, are saved in your device's local storage so the app remembers them between sessions. They stay on your device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else, and they are removed when you clear the app's data.

We do not collect, store, or have access to this information.

Images from Wikipedia

All of the app's content ships inside it and runs offline. The only network request the app makes is to load illustrative images from Wikipedia / Wikimedia on demand. Those requests send no personal information, are governed by the Wikimedia Foundation's privacy policy, and the app works fully without them.

What we do not do

No advertising. No sale or sharing of personal data. No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. No user accounts. No collection of your data on any server we operate.

Children

The app collects no personal data, so it is safe for all ages. It is not directed at children in a way that collects information from them.
