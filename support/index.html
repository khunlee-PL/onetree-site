Four billion years of life — free, offline.

One Tree is a hand-built map of life on Earth. All of its content ships inside the app and works offline; the only thing that ever needs a connection is loading illustrative images from Wikipedia, and only when you ask for them.

Need help or have feedback? Email khunlee@gmail.com. Please include your device and iOS version so we can help faster.

Common questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole tree of life, the deep-time tour, the games and every panel are stored in the app and work with no connection. The only network use is loading illustrative photographs from Wikipedia on demand, and the app is fully usable without them.

What are the two ways to explore?

Walk the Tree starts you as Homo sapiens and walks you back down your own branch, meeting your ancestors one divergence at a time, then back up to meet their other children — with games to trace your kinship with a lemur, an octopus or an oak, and a quiz. Tour Deep Time is a rotating globe and a radial clade map you scrub from the Big Bang to now, watching the tree grow outward from a single cell.

Are the ancestors real photographs?

No — and the app never pretends they are. Reconstructed ancestors are shown as exactly that: inferred from their living descendants. Living species, inferred ancestors and fossils are kept visually distinct, so a reconstruction never reads as a record.

How accurate is the timeline?

Every date sits in the geological period it actually happened in, every ancestor is older than its descendants, and where the science is uncertain the uncertainty is shown rather than hidden.

Is it really free?

Yes — no ads, no subscriptions, no accounts, and no tracking.
